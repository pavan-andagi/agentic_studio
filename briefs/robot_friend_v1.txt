Emotional 60-second vertical short. A small girl opens a gift box and a small original robot pops out; it becomes her friend and caretaker for life. Robot design, identical in every shot: small round white robot, one large round soft-blue light eye, rounded body, short arms, little antenna with an amber tip; original design, must not resemble WALL-E, Baymax, EVE or any known robot. Recurring object: a red ribbon the girl ties on its antenna. Show the girl mostly from behind, side, silhouette or hands (no face close-ups); show her age through height, hair and clothes. Beats: 1) COLD OPEN curiosity hook: dusty storage room, an old wrinkled hand wipes dust off a dark robot eye. 2) Happy: gift box, robot pops out, eye lights up, she ties the ribbon. 3) Montage: robot helps with homework, plays with her, listens to her, comforts her when she cries, cares for her when she is sick in bed. 4) Bittersweet: she grows up, a simple wedding silhouette, she gets busy with family, walks past the robot without looking. 5) Sad: robot switched off, put in a box in a storage room, dust and years passing in window light, ribbon fading. 6) Payoff: she is very old, opens the storage room, wipes the dust (matching the opening), switches it on, the eye flickers to life; final on-screen text: Hello, my old friend. End on the glowing eye and faded ribbon so it loops to the opening. Very sparse narration (max 4 short lines) or none; music carries the emotion: gentle music box and piano, warm, turning melancholic, ending tender. Wide and medium shots, slow camera moves, warm soft light.
